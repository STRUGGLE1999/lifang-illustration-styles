# Handwritten Lecture Notes Landscape

## Identity

- **Slug:** `handwritten-lecture-notes-landscape`
- **Best for:** Horizontal blog illustrations, article section diagrams, workshop-style explanation sheets, before/after comparisons.
- **Mood:** Calm, analytical, editorial, handwritten, practical, low-saturation.
- **Reference assets:** `assets/styles/handwritten-lecture-notes-landscape/references/`

## Non-Negotiable Traits

- Landscape note-sheet composition, usually 16:9 or 4:3.
- Warm off-white paper background with a lightly photographed, real-paper feel.
- Large blue-gray handwritten title across the top-left or top band, with a long hand-drawn underline.
- Main content uses black handwritten Chinese notes, blue-gray boxes/arrows/dividers, and pale yellow highlighter on key phrases.
- One clear horizontal explanation structure: old/new comparison, process flow, roadmap, triangle model, or cause-to-result diagram.
- Right-top area may be blank or contain only a tiny decorative star/underline, but never a label naming the format.
- The image should support a blog article as a horizontal handwritten explainer, not become a vertical card or polished slide.

## Flexible Traits

- Layout may use left/right comparison, center arrow, horizontal flow, or three-zone explanation.
- Text density should be lower than the portrait version, with more breathing room.
- Numbered bullets, side annotations, and summary boxes are allowed when they clarify the concept.
- Small decorative doodles are optional and should remain sparse.

## Visual System

### Composition

- Default canvas is 16:9; use 4:3 for a more compact article illustration.
- Keep a strong top title line and one dominant reading path across the page.
- For article ideas, prefer:
  - left old-rule box, center transformation arrow, right new-rule box;
  - bottom horizontal flowchart;
  - bottom-right takeaway box.
- Leave enough blank paper around diagrams to preserve the photographed-note feeling.

### Forms and Characters

- Avoid character illustration by default.
- Use hand-drawn boxes, arrows, circles, checklists, sticky-note-like labels, star doodles, and underline notes.
- Forms should be slightly imperfect and marker-like, not vector-clean.

### Linework and Texture

- Black pen for body handwriting.
- Blue-gray marker for title, structural lines, arrows, boxes, and annotations.
- Pale yellow highlighter behind short phrases only.
- Keep shadows and paper texture subtle, like a photographed note sheet.

### Palette and Emphasis

- Base: warm off-white paper.
- Main ink: black or charcoal.
- Structure ink: muted blue-gray / navy-gray.
- Highlight: pale yellow.
- Avoid colorful infographic palettes, gradients, and glossy effects.

### Typography and Annotations

- All visible text should look handwritten and remain readable.
- Use short labels and compact notes; avoid long paragraphs in the horizontal version.
- Do not write format labels such as `横版讲义`, `竖版讲义`, `landscape`, or `portrait` anywhere in the image.
- Top-right should not contain a format label; use blank space, a small star, or a decorative underline only.

## Repeated Motifs

- Long blue-gray title underline, comparison boxes, transformation arrow, yellow-highlighted key terms, horizontal flowchart, handwritten side labels, star doodles, bottom summary box.

Keep the style calm, analytical, low-saturation, and article-friendly, like a real workshop note photographed from above. The top-right corner should be blank or decorative only, not a label.

## Negative Constraints

```text
Do not create a vertical note page, colorful social-media card, cute cartoon infographic, glossy slide, 3D scene, anime style, or dense poster. Do not write format labels such as "横版讲义", "竖版讲义", "landscape", "portrait", "horizontal", or "vertical" anywhere in the image. Do not add a top-right page badge or format note unless explicitly requested by the user.
```
