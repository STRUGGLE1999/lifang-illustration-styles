# Warm Handdrawn Blog Illustration

## Identity

- **Slug:** `warm-handdrawn-blog-illustration`
- **Best for:** Blog article hero images, horizontal in-article explainers, newsletter illustrations, WeChat/Zhihu/CSDN article section visuals.
- **Mood:** Warm, thoughtful, clear, editorial, lightly playful, practical.
- **Reference assets:** `assets/styles/warm-handdrawn-card-series/references/`

## Non-Negotiable Traits

- Horizontal editorial illustration format, usually 16:9 for hero images or 4:3 for in-article illustrations.
- Warm off-white paper background with subtle hand-drawn border, soft texture, and small sparkles or motion marks.
- Thick dark coffee-brown hand-drawn outlines with soft colored-pencil fills.
- Large readable Chinese title or short phrase, usually placed on the left or top-left; one key term may be highlighted in warm orange.
- A central metaphor scene instead of a vertical page-card: people, laptop, robot, documents, tools, road signs, charts, gears, bridge, doorway, or workflow objects.
- Optional small annotation tags, arrows, dotted paths, tape labels, or mini callout cards to explain the idea without turning the image into a dense poster.
- Warm palette: cream, pale beige, carrot orange, sage green, dusty blue, honey yellow, and dark coffee-brown ink.

## Flexible Traits

- Text can be minimal for blog hero images, or slightly richer for section explainers.
- Layout may be left-title/right-scene, center-scene with small labels, or three-part horizontal journey.
- Numbered badges are optional; use them only when explaining a sequence.
- Footer takeaway strips and page badges are usually omitted unless the user asks for a card-like series.
- Characters, icons, and metaphors should adapt to the article, while preserving the same warm handmade visual language.

## Visual System

### Composition

- Default canvas: 16:9 horizontal blog image. Use 4:3 when the user asks for a compact article illustration.
- Keep generous margins and leave breathing room around the main metaphor.
- Prefer one clear focal scene over many equal panels.
- Common structures:
  - Left headline plus right metaphor scene.
  - Central workplace scene with 3 to 5 small labeled objects around it.
  - Horizontal before/after or old/new comparison with a soft arrow between them.
  - Roadmap scene with a path, milestones, and friendly icons.

### Forms and Characters

- Characters are simple workplace figures with rounded faces, calm expressions, and minimal features.
- Objects are simplified doodles: laptop, robot, sticky notes, resume, code window, toolbox, compass, calendar, chart, target, rocket, paper airplane, light bulb.
- Use rounded labels, tape banners, pill tags, dashed arrows, and soft callout cards.
- Keep perspective shallow and friendly; avoid realistic depth or complex backgrounds.

### Linework and Texture

- Use slightly wobbly dark-brown marker outlines.
- Fills should look matte, soft, and hand-colored.
- Add small underline swashes, sparkles, radiating lines, and dotted paths for rhythm.
- Shadows should be faint and warm, never glossy or dramatic.

### Palette and Emphasis

- Base: warm ivory paper and pale cream.
- Ink: deep coffee brown or charcoal.
- Accents: carrot orange for the key idea, sage green for calm/positive signals, dusty blue for AI/technology, honey yellow for highlights.
- Avoid cold corporate blue dominance; the image should feel editorial and handmade.

### Typography and Annotations

- Chinese title should be hand-lettered, bold, and readable at blog width.
- Keep body annotations short: 2 to 6 Chinese characters per label where possible.
- Use English only as small secondary labels, not as the main communication layer.
- Do not overload the image with paragraph text; blog illustrations should support the article, not replace it.

## Repeated Motifs

- Warm paper background, hand-drawn border, tape labels, orange keyword emphasis, dotted arrows, sparkles, smiling laptop, friendly robot, paper airplane, light bulb, charts, checklist, toolbox, simple workplace characters, small metaphor objects.

## Base Prompt

```text
Create a horizontal Chinese blog illustration in a warm hand-drawn editorial style, 16:9 aspect ratio unless another ratio is requested. Use an off-white textured paper background with a subtle rounded hand-drawn border. Draw with thick, slightly imperfect dark coffee-brown marker outlines and soft colored-pencil fills. Build one clear metaphor scene for the article idea, using simple workplace characters, laptop, AI robot, documents, charts, tools, arrows, dotted paths, tape labels, and small friendly doodle icons as needed. Place a short readable Chinese title or key phrase on the left or top-left, with one key term highlighted in warm orange. Keep the layout airy, warm, thoughtful, and article-friendly. Use cream, pale beige, carrot orange, sage green, dusty blue, honey yellow, and dark brown ink. Add small sparkles, underline swashes, and motion marks, but avoid dense card grids unless specifically requested.
```

## Negative Constraints

```text
Do not generate a vertical 9:16 or 3:4 social card unless explicitly requested. Do not include a top-right 01/05 page badge by default. Do not use dense 2x2 numbered panels as the main layout. Avoid photorealism, 3D, glossy UI, cold corporate tech slides, cyberpunk, anime-heavy characters, tiny unreadable paragraph text, exact FDE wording, copied signatures, watermarks, or logos.
```

- [ ] One clear central metaphor scene rather than a vertical card grid.
- [ ] Warm paper background and handmade border.
- [ ] Dark-brown marker outlines with soft pencil-like fills.
- [ ] Short Chinese title or key phrase is readable and not too large for the scene.
- [ ] Orange emphasis is used on one important concept only.
- [ ] Small labels and arrows clarify the idea without becoming a poster.
- [ ] No page badge or carousel marker unless requested.
- [ ] The image keeps the same warm hand-drawn language as `warm-handdrawn-card-series` while using a horizontal editorial layout.
