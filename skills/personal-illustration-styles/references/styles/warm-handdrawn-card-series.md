# Warm Handdrawn Card Series

## Identity

- **Slug:** `warm-handdrawn-card-series`
- **Best for:** Chinese knowledge cards, article illustration series, workplace/AI/product explainers, social-media carousel images.
- **Mood:** Warm, approachable, structured, lightly playful, practical and encouraging.
- **Reference assets:** `assets/styles/warm-handdrawn-card-series/references/`

## Non-Negotiable Traits

- Vertical card format with warm off-white paper background, rounded outer border, and a small stitched page badge such as `01/05` in the top-right corner.
- Large hand-lettered Chinese headline in dark coffee-brown marker strokes, with one key term highlighted in warm orange.
- Cream rounded content panels arranged as a 2x2 grid, stacked rows, or a dense hybrid layout; panels use pale yellow borders and soft shadow.
- Numbered circular badges in a recurring four-color system: carrot orange, sage green, dusty blue, and warm yellow.
- Friendly doodle icons: smiling laptop, rocket, paper airplane, star, light bulb, chart, checklist, gear, robot, people talking, toolbox, target, trophy.
- Hand-drawn linework is slightly imperfect and dark brown/charcoal, with soft colored-pencil fills and small sparkles or motion marks.
- Bottom takeaway strip with a star or paper airplane and a concise handwritten closing sentence.

## Flexible Traits

- The exact panel count may vary from 3 to 6 depending on the article structure.
- Layout can be 2x2, 5 stacked bands, or a top-summary plus flow strip, as long as the reading path remains clear.
- Icons should adapt to the subject and should not repeat FDE-specific objects unless relevant.
- English subtitle labels are optional; when used, keep them short and pair with Chinese, for example `Career Reset | 从简历到作品`.
- The page badge may use `01/05`, `02/05`, etc. for a series, or be omitted for a single standalone card.

## Visual System

### Composition

- Use a clean vertical poster/card canvas, ideally 3:4 or 4:5.
- Top zone: small guiding sentence, oversized title, optional side doodles, and beige tape-style subtitle banner.
- Middle zone: numbered explanation panels with short headings, two to four lines of body text, and one supporting doodle per panel.
- Lower zone: optional process strip, tag chips, or icon sequence.
- Footer: dashed or lightly bordered rounded takeaway strip with a memorable sentence.

### Forms and Characters

- Characters are simple, friendly office/workplace figures with rounded faces, soft expressions, and minimal detail.
- Objects are simplified and iconic rather than realistic: laptops, documents, gears, charts, calendars, databases, rockets, checklists, arrows.
- Use rounded rectangles, tape banners, pill tags, circular number badges, and dotted paths.
- Avoid complex perspective; most objects are front-facing or slight three-quarter doodles.

### Linework and Texture

- Draw with thick, slightly wobbly marker outlines in dark brown or charcoal.
- Fill areas with matte colored-pencil or watercolor-like flat color; keep texture soft and handmade.
- Use underline swashes, small radiating lines, sparkles, dashed separators, and curved arrows to create energy.
- Keep shadows faint and warm; no glossy gradients or 3D lighting.

### Palette and Emphasis

- Base: warm ivory, cream, pale beige.
- Ink: deep coffee brown and charcoal black.
- Accent colors: carrot orange, muted sage green, dusty sky blue, honey yellow, soft peach.
- Use orange for the main keyword or strongest conclusion; use color sparingly so the card remains warm and readable.

### Typography and Annotations

- Chinese text should look hand-lettered but remain highly legible.
- Main headline uses thick marker strokes; panel headings use smaller bold handwritten text over colored brush swatches.
- Body text can be neat handwritten sans style; keep line length short.
- English phrases, if any, should be casual handwritten labels and secondary to Chinese.

## Repeated Motifs

- Top-right page badge, beige tape subtitle, numbered badges, rounded cream panels, sparkles, underlines, arrows, star footer, paper airplane, smiling device, rocket, checklist, gears, simple people-in-conversation scenes.

## Base Prompt

```text
Create a vertical Chinese knowledge-card infographic illustration in a warm hand-drawn marker style. Use an off-white paper background, softly rounded outer border, a small stitched page badge in the top-right, and a large dark coffee-brown handwritten Chinese title with one important keyword highlighted in warm orange. Add a beige tape-style subtitle banner. Arrange the content into rounded cream panels with pale yellow borders, soft shadows, and numbered circular badges in carrot orange, sage green, dusty blue, and warm yellow. Each panel should have a short handwritten Chinese heading, concise readable body text, and a simple friendly doodle icon or workplace character. Use slightly imperfect dark-brown ink outlines, soft colored-pencil fills, small sparkles, arrows, underlines, and motion marks. End with a dashed rounded takeaway strip containing a star or paper airplane and one memorable closing sentence. Keep the layout dense but airy, practical, warm, and social-media friendly.
```

## Negative Constraints

```text
Do not make the image photorealistic, 3D, glossy, cyberpunk, corporate slide-like, minimalist flat SaaS UI, anime-heavy, or overly polished. Avoid cold blue tech palettes, sharp geometric grids, tiny unreadable text, excessive decorative clutter, realistic portraits, copied signatures, watermarks, logos, or exact reproduction of reference text. Do not tie the style to the FDE topic unless the requested content is about FDE.
```
