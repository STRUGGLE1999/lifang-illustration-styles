# Handwritten Lecture Notes Portrait

## Identity

- **Slug:** `handwritten-lecture-notes-portrait`
- **Best for:** Vertical Chinese explainers, long note pages, lecture-style article illustrations, step-by-step learning notes.
- **Mood:** Calm, analytical, handwritten, practical, low-saturation, study-note-like.
- **Reference assets:** `assets/styles/handwritten-lecture-notes-portrait/references/`

## Non-Negotiable Traits

- Portrait note-page composition, usually 2:3 or A4-like vertical ratio.
- Warm off-white paper background with a lightly photographed paper feel.
- Mostly handwritten Chinese text in black ink, with blue-gray marker used for large titles, section dividers, arrows, boxes, circles, and annotations.
- Pale yellow highlighter marks only on important words or short phrases.
- Visuals are simple diagrams rather than illustrations: flow boxes, arrows, checklists, comparison cards, triangle models, side-note boxes, underlines, brackets.
- Sparse decorative doodles such as stars, light bulbs, arrows, wavy underlines, and small page markers like `1/4` when a series is requested.
- The image should feel like a real hand-drawn lecture note photographed from above, not a polished infographic.

## Flexible Traits

- The exact density may range from one clean page to a compact study sheet.
- Page markers are optional; use them only when the output is a numbered series.
- Layout may be paragraph-first, step-by-step, comparison, flowchart, or summary-note structure.
- Blue-gray can shift slightly toward navy, but the palette should remain restrained.
- Small supporting boxes may use very light gray-blue fills.

## Visual System

### Composition

- Use generous margins and natural note spacing.
- Put a large blue-gray handwritten title at the top with a straight or slightly imperfect underline.
- Organize content into sections separated by blue-gray lines, star bullets, arrows, or boxed headings.
- Use a bottom summary box or highlighted takeaway when the content needs a conclusion.
- Keep reading order top-to-bottom, like a page from a notebook.

### Forms and Characters

- Avoid character illustration unless the request specifically needs it.
- Prefer abstract note forms: boxes, arrows, circles, brackets, taped cards, checklists, and simple icons.
- Diagrams should look hand-drawn and practical, not vector-perfect.

### Linework and Texture

- Black handwritten body text should be slightly imperfect but readable.
- Blue-gray marker lines should be thicker than body text and visibly hand-drawn.
- Highlighter strokes should be translucent, uneven, and placed behind text.
- Paper should have subtle texture and slight warmth.

### Palette and Emphasis

- Base: warm off-white paper.
- Main ink: black or very dark charcoal.
- Structure ink: muted blue-gray / navy-gray.
- Highlight: pale yellow only, used sparingly.
- Avoid bright multi-color palettes.

### Typography and Annotations

- All text should look handwritten, not typeset.
- Chinese text must remain legible at viewing size.
- Titles are larger, blue-gray, and more marker-like.
- Body notes use black pen; annotations use blue-gray.
- Do not write format labels such as `竖版讲义`, `横版讲义`, `portrait`, or `landscape` anywhere in the image.

## Repeated Motifs

- Blue-gray top title underline, star bullet, yellow highlighter, hand-drawn boxes, arrows, flow steps, side-note boxes, checklist ticks, wavy underlines, bottom summary rectangle.
